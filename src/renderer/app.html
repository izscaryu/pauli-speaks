More ways to start

For pedals, Stream Deck, mice and scripts.
Double-tap a modifier
Press Add above, then tap Ctrl, Alt or Shift twice quickly - within 0.4 s, nothing else pressed. A double-tap always toggles.
Foot pedals and macro keys
Set the pedal or Stream Deck key to send F13-F24: no app uses them. A pedal that sends press and release separately works as Hold.
Mouse buttons
Middle click and the side buttons work as shortcuts, but they still do their usual job too (Back, Forward) - Windows doesn't let Pauli Speaks block them yet.
Links and command line
Point Stream Deck, AutoHotkey, foot-pedal software or a script at these. Links can only start and stop dictation, because any web page can open a link; pasting and key presses need the command line.

How it works

  1. A low-level keyboard hook watches for your shortcut in any app. It never blocks or records other keys.
  2. While you hold it, the microphone is recorded at 16 kHz.
  3. When you let go, Whisper turns speech into text on your own CPU, so nothing leaves your PC.
  4. Optionally the text is translated: Whisper's built-in translator (fast, English) or NLLB-200 (best, 30+ languages).
  5. Fillers, voice commands and your dictionary are applied, then the text is pasted with Ctrl+V and your clipboard is put back.

Shortcuts use exact key-combination matching, a 500 ms tap / double-tap window, hands-free lock, Esc to cancel and Start-menu masking for Ctrl+Win.
