Free voice typing for Windows

Hold a key, talk, let go. Your words are typed.

Pauli Speaks types what you say into any app on your PC. The speech recognition runs on your own computer, so it is free, it works offline and what you say stays with you.

“Windows protected your PC”?

When you run the installer, Windows may show a blue screen that says Windows protected your PC and Publisher: Unknown publisher. To install anyway:

  1. Click More info.
  2. Check that the file is Pauli-Speaks-Setup-<version>.exe.
  3. Click Run anyway.

This happens because the installer isn't code-signed yet. Microsoft Defender SmartScreen warns about any download that has no publisher signature or that few people have run. It doesn't mean a virus was found. Every installer is built from the public source code by GitHub Actions, so download it only from this site or from GitHub Releases. The code signing policy shows where signing stands.

What makes it different

Free, with no catch

No account, no subscription and no word limit. The source code is open, under the MIT licence.

Works offline

OpenAI's Whisper speech recognition runs on your PC, on the graphics card when there is one. The model downloads once; after that you don't need the internet.

Private

No analytics and no telemetry. Nothing you say leaves your PC unless you turn on a cloud option, and the app says so next to it. Privacy

Modes

A mode bundles language, translation, style and voice commands. Give each its own shortcut, or let it switch on by itself: formal in Outlook, code style in VS Code.

AI polish, on your PC first

Off by default. Light, Medium, High or Prompt for each mode, run on your PC with Ollama, LM Studio or Foundry Local. If it takes over 2.5 seconds, your words are pasted as you said them.

Your keys, pedals, Stream Deck

Hold to talk or double-tap for hands-free. Any key, an F13–F24 pedal or a mouse button, plus links and command-line flags. Shortcuts

Languages

Speak any of 99 languages

Whisper recognises 99 languages. Pick the ones you speak in the setup, and Pauli Speaks detects which of them you used. How well it works depends on the language and the model: Large v3 Turbo is the most accurate, Tiny the fastest.

Speak one language and paste another: Fast translates into English with Whisper itself, Best into 30+ languages with Meta's NLLB-200, also offline (about 900 MB, downloaded once).

The app in English and Croatian

The window, the setup, the pill and the tray menu are in English and Croatian (Hrvatski). A new language is one file, and you don't need to write code: translate Pauli Speaks.
