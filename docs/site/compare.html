Where others do better

  • Windows voice typing needs nothing installed and is on every Windows PC, in many languages (Croatian included). Voice access also lets you control the whole PC by voice.
  • Wispr Flow also runs on Mac, iPhone and Android, needs no setup for its clean-up, and has plans for teams.
  • Dragon has decades of work behind it: controlling the whole PC by voice, editions for specialised vocabulary (legal, for example), and support from a large company.
  • Handy and OpenWhispr are also free and open, and run on Mac and Linux too. Handy offers NVIDIA's Parakeet models; OpenWhispr does more than dictation. WhisperWriter (GPL-3.0) is another open option, for people comfortable with Python.

Where Pauli Speaks is weaker

  • Windows only.
  • The installer isn't code-signed yet, so Windows shows a SmartScreen warning.
  • A young project with one maintainer.
  • Local AI polish needs Ollama, LM Studio or Foundry Local and a PC that can run a model quickly; otherwise your words are pasted as said.
  • On an older PC without a graphics card, the larger Whisper models are slow.

What Pauli Speaks does its own way

  • Free, with the recognition on your PC by default, and no account.
  • Push-to-talk on any key, a pedal or a mouse button, plus links and command-line flags for Stream Deck and scripts.
  • Modes per app or per shortcut, translation while you speak, voice commands in eight languages, and an app in English and Croatian.
  • AI polish that runs on your PC first, never holds a dictation up, and can always be undone.
